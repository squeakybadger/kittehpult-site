Privacy

Last updated 23 September 2026

Kittehpult is a game about flinging cats. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server of its own. We never see your cats, your scores, or anything else you do in the game.

What stays on your device

Your designed cats, unlocks, high scores, settings and mode choice are stored on your phone by the app itself. Cat backups are written to your own device storage (on Android, the Downloads folder) so you can copy them somewhere else or move them to a new phone. Deleting the app deletes all of it; we have no copy.

Microphone

The cat designer can record a short clip of your own cat's meow. The game asks for microphone permission only when you tap the record button, and the recording is saved on your device — it is never uploaded anywhere. You can delete a recording, or the whole cat, at any time in the designer.

Advertising

The free version shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve them, AdMob receives technical information from your device such as a device advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and basic device and app details. That processing is Google's, described in the Google privacy & terms and the AdMob policies.

On iOS you are asked, through Apple's own App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether Kittehpult may use that identifier for tracking. Say no and ads still appear, just less targeted. You can change the answer later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, the equivalent control is Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Buying the in-app remove ads unlock stops the ads and the requests that go with them. The purchase itself is handled by Apple or Google; we receive no payment details.

Children

Kittehpult is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date above.
