How it goes

Pull, aim, release

Drag down to charge the kittehpult. The cat goes where you point it — mostly.

Stack to the finish

Cat beds steady the tower. Everything else makes it sway.

Donk the visitors

Pesky birds, aliens and asteroids come for your stack. Knock them away before they hit.

Autokitteh

Hit the bubble, press the button, and a geyser of cats raises the whole column.

Two ways to play

Casual is infinite lives and no stakes. Stressful is nine lives, tilt to steady, and the visitors bite.

Cat designer

Paint a cat, name it, and fling it. Your cats are saved on your phone and back up to a file you keep.
